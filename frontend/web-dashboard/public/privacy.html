Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information the MERCON Driver app, the MERCON Operator app and the MERCON web dashboard (together, “MERCON”) collect, why, and how it is protected. MERCON is a business tool used by MERCON Logistics Services Co. and its drivers and staff to run transport trips. It is not intended for the general public or for children.

1. Who can use MERCON

Accounts are created by MERCON’s office staff. Drivers sign in with the phone number and password given to them; office staff sign in with their username and password. Nobody can create an account from the app.

2. What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Location (precise and approximate)Only while a trip is running in the Driver app: to show the truck’s position to our dispatch team, detect arrivals and delays, and give customers arrival times. Tracking starts when the driver starts a trip, shows a permanent notification (“MERCON is sharing your trip location”) the whole time, and stops automatically when the trip is completed or cancelled, or when the driver logs out. We do not collect location when no trip is running.
Name, phone number, licence and ID detailsTo manage the driver’s account, assign trips and meet transport regulations.
Photos and videosTaken by the driver in the app as proof of cargo, delivery, delays or emergencies. Videos may include sound.
Documents (licence, ID, vehicle papers)Uploaded by the office to keep driver and vehicle records valid. Opened only through private links that expire.
Device information (push notification token, an app-install ID, phone model, app version, whether notifications and location are allowed, battery level)To send trip notifications to the right phone and to let the office see when a phone is not set up to receive trips.
Trip activity (status changes, times, messages, SOS alerts)To run and record the trip, pay drivers, and invoice customers.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or share information with third parties for their own marketing. The apps contain no ads.

4. Who receives the information

  • MERCON’s own staff (dispatch, operations, finance) who need it for their work.
  • The customer of a trip, who may receive a tracking link showing that trip’s progress and estimated arrival.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: our hosting provider, and Google Firebase / Apple Push Notification service / Expo for delivering notifications. They process data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, when required by Saudi law.

5. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access requires a password, and each person sees only what their role allows. Private documents are only reachable through signed links that expire.

6. How long we keep it

  • Trip photos and videos: deleted automatically about 60 days after the trip ends.
  • Trip records, invoices and driver payments: kept as long as required for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Account details: kept while the person works with MERCON, then deleted or anonymised unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Your choices and rights

You can turn off notifications or location for the app in your phone’s settings at any time; trip tracking and new-trip alerts will then not work. You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, by contacting us below. Some records may need to be kept by law.

8. Children

MERCON is for working adults only and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
