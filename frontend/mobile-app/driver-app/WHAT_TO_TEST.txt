NEW: TRIP LOG for customer-app trips (iMile, JD, Shipa app trips)
A. Start the trip on Home. You now get one "Trip log" screen instead of the step screens.
B. At every stop, tap "Send screenshots" and pick one or many screenshots. Each send shows with its time and place.
C. "Add more" adds forgotten screenshots to the last send; "Take back" removes a send made by mistake (within 15 minutes).
D. Finish trip when done. Report Delay still works from the top.
E. Normal trips keep the old step-by-step screens.
F. Trip log top: the planned route, done stops ticked, "Next: <place>". Screenshots show bigger.
